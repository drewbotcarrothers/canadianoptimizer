Explore Vanguard Asset Allocation ETFs

Since 1975, Vanguard has been guided by one simple philosophy: putting investors first. Building on a legacy of low-cost, broadly diversified index investing, Vanguard pioneered all-in-one Asset Allocation ETFs in Canada—setting a new industry standard.

That investor-first philosophy continues to shape how Vanguard designs investment solutions today and is at the core of our Asset Allocation ETFs. They are designed for different investing preferences, risk tolerances, and long-term investing goals, making it easier to find a portfolio aligned with your needs.

A diversified portfolio in one investment

Vanguard Asset Allocation ETFs are designed to provide investors with a complete, diversified portfolio through a single ETF.

Each portfolio is built from multiple underlying index ETFs across regions, sectors, and asset classes—and is automatically rebalanced to help maintain its intended mix over time.

For investors, this can help simplify portfolio management while reducing the need to build and maintain a diversified portfolio on their own.

Built around long-term investing principles

Vanguard’s approach to investing is grounded in enduring principles that have guided investors for decades: setting clear goals, staying diversified, keeping costs low, and maintaining long-term discipline.


These principles continue to shape how Vanguard Asset Allocation ETFs are designed today.

Clear investment goals

Explore portfolios designed for different risk tolerances, investment timelines, and long-term financial objectives.

Built-in diversification

Gain broad exposure across global markets and asset classes in a single ETF designed to help investors stay balanced over time.

Low-cost investing

Vanguard’s long-standing focus on low-cost investing help investors keep more of their returns invested over time.

Automatic rebalancing

Portfolios are automatically rebalanced to help maintain their intended asset mix and support long-term investing discipline through changing market conditions.
