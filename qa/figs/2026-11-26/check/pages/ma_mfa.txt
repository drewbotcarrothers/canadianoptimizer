Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

Emails related to multi-factor authentication (MFA)

You may have recently received an email notification from the CRA encouraging you to set up a backup MFA option in your CRA account. A backup method can help you if you forget or lose access to your primary MFA method. The CRA will not send you an email with a link asking you to sign in. Go to the latest scam alerts page for more details and information on scams and fake websites.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) increases the security of your CRA account by requiring you to enter a one-time passcode every time you sign in. MFA is mandatory for all CRA account users.

MFA options

When you register for a CRA account, we will prompt you to enroll in two MFA options to get your one-time passcodes. However, only one of the following is currently mandatory:

Third-party authenticator app: Use a mobile app that generates passcodes
Passcode grid: Save or print a passcode grid the CRA generates
Phone: Get passcodes sent to you by text or phone call

Get an authenticator app
Search for and download a free third-party authenticator app from a reputable app store

How to choose a reputable authenticator app

Most reputable, standard authenticator apps should be able to read the QR code we provide. To make sure the app you are downloading is reputable:

Download only from official app stores (such as Google Play, the Microsoft Store, or the Apple App Store)
Read the privacy agreement before you download to make sure your data is handled securely
Read recent reviews to check for reported security concerns
Check the number of reviews or downloads (a low number may be a concern)

You do not need to spend money to get a reputable authenticator app.

Learn how to recognize a scam.

Set up the app to use with your CRA account (one time only)
When you are setting up this MFA option for your CRA account, either:

Scan the provided QR code with a mobile device when prompted
Option 1 of 2

Manually enter the setup key the CRA provides to you into the app
Option 2 of 2

Get your passcode in the authenticator app
Every time you sign in to your CRA account and are prompted for a passcode, open your authenticator app to get the 6-digit code. For security, the app will generate a new passcode every 30 seconds.
Why we prompt you to enroll in two or more MFA options

Only one MFA option is mandatory right now. However, enrolling in multiple MFA options ensures that you can still access your CRA account if you:

Change your phone number
Can't access your text messages
Misplace your passcode grid
Delete the third-party authenticator app

If you have already enrolled with one option, you can add a backup MFA option in your CRA account.

Keep your passcode secure

You should never share your one-time passcode with anyone, including anyone calling or texting you and asking for it. The CRA will never call and ask you for your one-time passcode over the phone.

However, if you call us, you can choose to verify your identity during the call by providing your one-time passcode to:

Our Interactive Voice Response (IVR) system
The CRA service representative

You can also learn about How to recognize a scam.

Change your MFA settings

After you sign in to your CRA account, on the Welcome page you can:

Add or change a phone number for MFA
Add a new backup MFA option
Remove or change an MFA option
Generate a new passcode grid
Change your language settings
How to change your MFA options in your CRA account
Sign in to your CRA account
On the Welcome page select Security settings
Choose Multi-factor authentication then Continue
Choose the MFA option you want to add, remove, or change:
Telephone: Add, edit or delete phone numbers, and change your preferred language
Passcode grid: Add, view or delete a passcode grid
Authenticator app: Add or remove an authenticator app
If you can't receive your one-time passcode

If your phone number has changed or you can no longer receive your one-time passcode to that number, you can use another MFA option you are already enrolled with.

If you still cannot receive your one-time passcode, contact us.

If you are locked out for entering the wrong passcode too many times

If you enter the wrong one-time passcode too many times, you will be temporarily locked out of your CRA account for 30 minutes. After that period, you can try again or use another method you are enrolled with. If this happens 3 times, you will be permanently locked out and will need to contact us.
