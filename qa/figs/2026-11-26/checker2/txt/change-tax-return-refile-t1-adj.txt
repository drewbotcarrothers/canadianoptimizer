When can you ask the CRA to change your return?
    
Only after you receive your notice of assessment. The CRA's Changing a tax return page says the return must have been filed by you or your tax preparer, and that if you already submitted a change request you must wait for the CRA's response before you ask for more changes.
    
The second rule matters more than it looks. If you find one error today and a second one next week, you cannot just send another request while the first is open. Collect everything first. The CRA's own tip is to include all changes in a single request and double-check the amounts.

    
What can you change, and what can you not?
    
You can correct amounts and add missing information. The CRA lists three examples: reporting income such as a T4 slip or tips, claiming a deduction, credit or expense such as childcare or medical costs, and adding a missing tax slip such as an RRSP contribution receipt.

Do you have to send supporting documents?

How long does a change take, really?

Is this a change request or an objection?
    
If information was missing or entered wrongly, ask for a change. If the information is complete but you disagree with the CRA's assessment or reassessment, that is an objection, a formal dispute. The CRA's notice of assessment page gives you 90 days from the date of your notice to register one.
    
The CRA's income tax objections decision tree adds that an individual's deadline is whichever is later: one year after the filing deadline for the return (April 30 or June 15), or 90 days after the date on the notice. It also says that if you filed a request to change your return and have not heard back, it may be faster to wait for the CRA to finish the adjustment. If you missed the objection deadline because you were trying to have your return adjusted, you may apply for an extension of up to a year after the objection deadline.

    
Frequently asked questions
    
Can I file a second tax return to fix a mistake?
    
No. Ask the CRA to change the assessed return through Change my return, ReFILE or Form T1-ADJ. You must wait for your notice of assessment first.
    
What is the difference between ReFILE and T1-ADJ?
    
ReFILE is the online change service inside certified tax software, listed at 2 weeks. T1-ADJ is the paper form, listed at 20 weeks.
    
Do I need to attach receipts to an online change request?
    
Not at first. The CRA says it will contact you if it needs documents, and give you a case number for submitting them online.
    
Can I change my address or direct deposit on my tax return?
    
No. The CRA says a change request cannot update your address, direct deposit information, marital status or name. Make those changes in your CRA account.
    
Can I ask for a second change while the first is pending?
    
No. The CRA says to wait for its response to the first request before asking for more changes, which is why you should put every correction in one request.
    
What if I left out income years ago?
    
Consider the Voluntary Disclosures Program, which can reduce penalties in many cases. The CRA's page says you can apply at any time of year if the mistake is at least one year past the filing due date.
