T1-ADJ T1 Adjustment Request
Faster processing for online adjustment requests

Using the webform or paper form to request an adjustment may take 20 weeks to process due to high volumes. For faster service, you may be able to change your tax return online in your CRA account or by using tax software (ReFILE).

Use this form to request an adjustment (reassessment) to a personal income tax return.

All options below allow you to print and mail your request to the CRA.

For faster service, you may be able to change your tax return online in your CRA account or by using tax software (ReFILE).
